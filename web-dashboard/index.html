How GhostTrack Detects Apps Before It Was Installed

Android maintains a persistent, low-level service named usagestats. Even when an application is uninstalled from the phone, Android preserves historical screen time, launch events (ACTIVITY_RESUMED), and timestamps in the system database. GhostTrack queries this OS-level log and cross-checks every record against currently installed packages to unmask ghost apps.
